# Add your TreasureBoat ID

Enter your **TreasureBoat ID** here.

![TB ID Input](/assets/S2M/Mobile/Developer/TBIdInput.jpeg)

And click on the Save Button at the end of the screen.

After successfully adding the ID, you can find now below the Save Button a new  
**Remove TreasureBoat ID** Button
