# TreasureBoat ID Tools

![Special Menu](/assets/S2M/Mobile/Developer/TBDevMenu.png)

* License – You can see your Developer License (Developer only)
* Approve App – You can approve and link other App TreasureBoat Apps (macOS, AppleTV, visionOS)
* TBConnect – Displaying useful Links
