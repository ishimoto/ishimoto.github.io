# Two Factors Authentication – Login

A normal TB Login Form has usually 3 Fields to enter the credentials.  
The first one is the Username, the second one is the Password and the third one is the Two-Factor Authentication Code.

![Login Form](/assets/S2M/TwoFactor/LoginForm.png)

If the Two-Factor Authentication is not switched ON then only the 2 first fields are mandatory.  
After the Two-Factor Authentication is ON the user needs to add the one-time token that change every 30 seconds.

Check your Device for the Authenticator App, and add the one-time token.
